# What's new in Postfold

Every release, newest first. Version numbers match Settings › About in the app.

## Version 1.7

November 24, 2026 · iPhone, iPad and Android

### Work with many posts at once

- Tap **Select**, pick posts or **Select All**, then:
  - **Delete** them from Postfold, out of every folder.
  - **Move to Folder** to file them somewhere else.
  - **Add to Folder** to put them in more folders and keep the ones they're in.
  - **Remove from Folder**, inside a folder, to take them out of that folder only.
- Postfold asks before deleting or removing and shows how many posts are affected. You can create a new folder right from the folder picker.

### Revisit reminders

- Turn on **Revisit Reminders** in Settings to get a gentle nudge about saved posts you haven't opened in a while: after 3 days up to 6 months, at the time you choose.
- The new **To Revisit** list shows those posts. Turn reminders off for any folder you don't need them for.

### Improvements

- Previews for YouTube, Reddit and Facebook posts work again.
- A post whose preview can't load says so, instead of loading forever.

## Version 1.6

November 21, 2026 · iPhone, iPad and Android

### Import and export

- Back up your whole library, one folder, or the posts you select, as a **Postfold Backup (JSON)** or **HTML Bookmarks** file.
- **Import** a Postfold backup, bookmarks from your browser, a CSV file or a plain list of links. Posts you already have aren't saved twice.

### Sync

- iPhone and iPad: **Settings › iCloud Sync** shows your iCloud account status and when your library last synced, and lets you turn sync on or off.
- Android: Settings explains how your library is kept in your device's Google backup.

### Improvements

- **Rate Postfold** from Settings.
- Bug fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.5

November 20, 2026 · iPhone, iPad and Android

### Save posts from more than X

- Save posts from **Threads, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit and LinkedIn**, in addition to X. Share a post from any of these apps, or paste its link.
- Each post shows its platform's official embed on the details screen, and one tap opens it in that platform's app.
- YouTube videos show their thumbnail in the post list.
- **Any other link** can now be saved as a Link Only item, with your own title, note and folders. LinkedIn posts are saved this way too.
- New **Platform** filter in the post list. The Author filter now lists authors per platform.
- Posts that a platform won't show anymore (deleted, private or removed) are marked Unavailable, and your link, title, note and folders are kept.

### Improvements

- The same post is never saved twice: saving it again adds the new folders to the post you already have.
- Android: embedded posts on the details screen now get the right height.

## Version 1.2

November 18, 2026 · iPhone, iPad and Android

### One post, many folders

- A post can now live in **more than one folder**. Tick as many folders as you like when you save a post, from the Add screen or the Share sheet.
- **Folders**, in a post's menu or on its details screen, replaces Move to Folder, so you can add or remove folders in one place.
- Untick every folder to keep a post in Unsorted.
- Saving a post you already have adds the folders you picked instead of moving it.
- Your existing library is upgraded automatically. Every post stays in the folder it was in.

## Version 1.1

November 10, 2026 · iPhone and iPad

- Postfold is now supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the post details screen, an ad between posts in the list, and an occasional full-screen ad after you add posts. Ads never see your posts, notes or folders.
- Choose how ads use your data with Google's consent form and the iOS tracking prompt. Change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy Options.

## Android 1.0

November 10, 2026 · Android

The first release for Android phones and tablets, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.postfold).

- Everything from the iPhone and iPad app: folders with emoji and colors, custom titles and notes, sorting, filters, search and three list layouts.
- Save from the Android Share sheet, or copy a post link and paste it in Postfold.
- One, two or three columns on tablets and in landscape.
- Your library stays on your device and is included in your Android backup.
- Crash reports and usage statistics through Google Firebase, with an opt-out in Settings. See the [Privacy Policy](https://postfold.space/privacy/).

## Version 1.0

November 7, 2026 · iPhone and iPad

The first release of Postfold.

- **Save in two taps** from the X app's Share sheet: pick a folder, add a note, done. Or copy a post link and tap Paste on the banner in Postfold.
- **Folders** with their own emoji and color, and a live post count.
- **Custom titles and private notes** for every saved post.
- **Sort** by date added, date modified, title, date posted or author. **Filter** by date range, author, note or status. **Search** post text, authors, titles and notes.
- **Three layouts**: Preview, Compact and Grid.
- **Private iCloud sync** across your iPhone and iPad. No login, no account, no server.
- Deleted or private posts are marked **Unavailable**, and your link, title, note and folder are kept.
- Saved posts are checked again in the background, so your library stays up to date.
- Anonymous crash reports through Apple's built-in tools, with an opt-out in Settings.
